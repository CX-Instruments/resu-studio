# Events and Facilities Officer

**River City Arts Centre** (fictional, for testing)  
Brisbane QLD, full time

## The role

Coordinate venue bookings, event schedules, contractors and day-to-day facility needs for a busy community arts centre.

## What we need

- At least three years of experience in events or venue operations
- Experience coordinating suppliers and contractors
- Strong scheduling and budget tracking skills
- Confidence using Microsoft Excel
- Clear written and verbal communication

Experience with venue safety procedures is desirable.

To apply, send your CV and a short cover letter.
