# Operations Coordinator

**Northside Community Health Service** (fictional, for testing)
Brisbane QLD, full time, permanent

## About the role

Northside Community Health runs six clinics across Brisbane's north. The Operations Coordinator keeps those clinics running: rosters, facilities, suppliers and the systems that tie them together. You will report to the Operations Manager and work closely with clinic team leaders.

## Key responsibilities

- Coordinate rosters for administrative and support staff across six sites
- Manage contracts and service levels with cleaning, security and maintenance suppliers
- Monitor the operations budget and report variances monthly
- Maintain work health and safety records and coordinate incident reporting
- Improve operational processes and document them for the team

## Selection criteria

**Essential**

1. Demonstrated experience coordinating rosters for a team of 20 or more
2. Experience managing supplier contracts and service levels
3. Budget monitoring and reporting skills
4. Knowledge of work health and safety incident reporting
5. Current First Aid certificate

**Desirable**

1. Experience in health or community services
2. Experience with Xero or similar finance software

Applicants must hold a current Working with Children Check (Blue Card) or be eligible to obtain one.

To apply, send a CV and a cover letter of no more than one page that addresses the essential criteria.
