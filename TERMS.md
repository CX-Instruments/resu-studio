## What it does

Resu Studio helps you tailor a CV and cover letter to a job advertisement. It scores
what the advertisement asks for against what you have told it about your working
life, proposes changes for you to accept or reject, and prints the documents you
approve.

## Your content and your decisions

- Your CV, your answers and your finished documents are yours. Resu Studio does not
  collect them. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
- Every change is a suggestion. You decide what goes into a document, and you are
  responsible for what you send to an employer.
- Only give it information that is yours to use, and check that every claim in a
  finished document is accurate before you send it.
- Resu Studio is a writing tool. It is not careers, legal or migration advice, and it
  cannot tell you whether you will be shortlisted or hired.
