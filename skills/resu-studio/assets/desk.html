What this page is

A list of every job you are applying for, with where each one is up to, when it closes, how well your CV answers the ad, and the documents made for it. It is rebuilt every time the AI works on one of your jobs.

To update a job

  1. On the job, open Update this application and change the stage, the closing date, or add a note.
  2. Press Hand to AI, then Copy.
  3. Paste it into your chat with the AI. It updates your records and gives you a fresh Resu Desk.

If a job changed after this page was built, the AI tells you and asks which is right. Nothing is written over.

The buttons on a job

Open Studio opens that job's Studio: your CV scored against the ad, where you accept or reject suggested changes. CV PDF and Cover letter PDF open the finished documents. Each opens in a new browser tab, so this page stays open. The line under them says which folder they are in.

If a button opens a file view instead of a browser tab, this page is being shown inside another program, such as a code editor. Open Resu Desk.html from your folder by double-clicking it, so it opens in Chrome, Edge or your usual browser.
